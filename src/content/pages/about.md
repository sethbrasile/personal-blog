To some extent, this is just my [(Seth Brasile)](/authors/seth-brasile) personal blog. I write about stuff for my youtube channels, software development, programming languages, and computer science. I also write about things that generally interest me such as general technology, science, music production, and making stuff.

Sometimes some of my friends might write guest posts. I'm interested in new contributors, so if you're interested in writing a guest post, please reach out to me.

## Other projects

[Graphic Tees Co.](https://graphicteesco.com/) is a tee and hoodie shop I build and run with PPMC. The designs are ours, and the website is an Astro app in front of a Shopify store.

[Potluck Planner](https://potluck-planner.com/) is a potluck app I came up with to suit how my family likes to handle holidays. Eventually I decided other people might like to use something similar. A host makes the event and sends a link. Guests RSVP and claim a dish from that link, with no account required.

[Cathode](https://cathodeaudio.com/) is a guitar amp app, and it is not out yet. The app is C++. It simulates the circuit, and the power stage is a neural model. At its core, it isn't just a guitar amp app, it will be able to simulate any electronic circuit and let you pass audio through it, but the target is a nice experience for people designing amps and pedals.
