To some extent, this is just my [(Seth Brasile)](/authors/seth-brasile) personal blog. I write about stuff for my youtube channels, software development, programming languages, and computer science. I also write about things that generally interest me such as general technology, science, music production, and making stuff.

Sometimes some of my friends might write guest posts. I'm interested in new contributors, so if you're interested in writing a guest post, please reach out to me.

## Other projects

[Graphic Tees Co.](https://graphicteesco.com/) is a tee and hoodie shop I build and run with PPMC. The designs are ours, Printful prints them, and the website is an Astro app in front of a Shopify store.

[Potluck Planner](https://potluck-planner.com/) is a potluck app I came up with and still run. A host makes the event and sends a link. Guests RSVP and claim a dish from that link, with no password account. Agents wrote most of the code. I review it, and I own the architecture.

[Cathode](https://cathodeaudio.com/) is a guitar amp app, and it is not out yet. The app is C++. It simulates the circuit, and the power stage is a neural model. I direct the build. Claude writes most of the code.
